# HEOR Uncertainty Analysis

Create a reproducible local uncertainty artifact; do not generate ad hoc simulation code. Read `references/uncertainty-contract.md` before creating or changing the artifact.

## Workflow

1. Read `heor/analysis-plan.json`, its `input_provenance`, and `heor/conceptual-model.json`. Use the current exact bytes, not a remembered plan.
2. Confirm a positive willingness-to-pay value and a fixed `uncertainty_analysis.path` of `heor/uncertainty-plan.json` in the analysis plan.
3. Select parameter targets only from the engine allowlist. Link each target to one `distribution_available` provenance mapping. Do not invent a range, distribution, or dependence assumption from a point estimate. Pair analysis/uncertainty `0.8.0`/`0.7.0`, background mortality `0.9.0`/`0.8.0`, relative effect `0.10.0`/`0.9.0`, and constant HR `0.11.0`/`0.10.0` exactly. Partitioned-survival analysis `0.12.0` pairs with economic-only uncertainty `0.11.0` or joint survival uncertainty `0.12.0`. Earlier schemas remain readable for compatible legacy analyses.
4. Record one-way low/high values that bracket the base value and explain their evidence basis. Use coherent complete transition rows rather than changing a single probability independently; scheduled rows use `/strategies/<role>/transition_schedule/<phase>/matrix/<row>`.
5. Choose an evidence-compatible distribution. Record basis IDs from the linked provenance mapping and explain the parameterization. Use Dirichlet for a complete transition row.
6. For an admitted constant competing-rate transformation in legacy analysis schema `0.5.0`, or analysis `0.8.0` paired with uncertainty `0.7.0`, target only one exact event rate at `/input_provenance/<mapping>/derivation/transformation/phases/<phase>/rows/<row>/events/<event>/rate_per_year`. Require positive DSA bounds and gamma, lognormal, or strictly positive uniform PSA values. Bind `basis_ids` to exactly that event's extraction or proposed assumption. The engine recomputes the affected complete matrix or schedule after all rate draws; never target its derived probability row.
7. For an admitted survival transformation in legacy analysis schema `0.6.0`, use uncertainty schema `0.5.0`; for analysis `0.8.0`, use uncertainty `0.7.0`. Target only `/input_provenance/<mapping>/derivation/transformation/parameters/<parameter>/value`, where `<parameter>` is exponential `rate_per_year` or Weibull `shape` or `scale_years`. Require positive DSA bounds, gamma, lognormal, or strictly positive uniform PSA, and exactly the parameter's extraction or proposed-assumption basis. After all replacements, the engine recomputes the complete per-cycle schedule once per affected mapping. Treat alternative curve families as structural uncertainty outside this parameter target.
8. For an admitted probability-time transformation in legacy analysis schema `0.7.0`, use uncertainty schema `0.6.0`; for analysis `0.8.0`, use uncertainty `0.7.0`. Target only `/input_provenance/<mapping>/derivation/transformation/phases/<phase>/rows/<row>/event/source_probability`. Require DSA bounds strictly inside `(0,1)`, Beta or Uniform with both bounds strictly inside `(0,1)`, and exactly the source probability's extraction or proposed-assumption basis. Recompute the complete matrix or schedule after all replacements; never target its derived probability row.
9. For analysis `0.9.0` with uncertainty `0.8.0`, every parameter target must be exactly `/input_provenance/<mapping>/derivation/transformation/excess_mortality_rate_per_year/value`. Require strictly positive DSA bounds, Gamma, Lognormal, or strictly positive Uniform PSA, and exactly the excess-rate basis. Keep `life_table`, `review_bases`, `operation`, and all other transformation internals fixed. Additive-versus-multiplicative mortality remains a Human-in-the-loop structural limitation, not an executable scenario replacement.
10. For analysis `0.10.0` with uncertainty `0.9.0`, target only `/input_provenance/<mapping>/derivation/transformation/relative_effect/value` and bind exactly its basis. RR requires positive DSA bounds with high strictly below `1/max(baseline q>0)` and bounded Uniform PSA with the same strict ceiling; reject Gamma, Lognormal, or any unbounded RR support. OR permits positive DSA bounds plus Lognormal or strictly positive Uniform PSA. Keep baselines, measure, intervals, review bases, operation, and other internals fixed.
11. For analysis `0.11.0` with uncertainty `0.10.0`, target only `/input_provenance/<mapping>/derivation/transformation/hazard_ratio/value` and bind exactly its basis. Require positive DSA bounds and a strictly positive bounded Uniform PSA. Both high bounds must reproduce a finite complete schedule from the fixed baseline cumulative-hazard increments. Keep all baseline hazards, review bases, indices, operation, and other internals fixed. Unbounded Lognormal support is deferred until an auditable truncated distribution exists.
12. For partitioned-survival analysis `0.12.0` with uncertainty `0.11.0`, read `heor/partitioned-survival-plan.json`, `heor/survival-curve-materializations.json`, and `heor/treatment-effect-duration.json` through `$heor-partitioned-survival` and `$heor-treatment-effect-duration`, and read structure-neutral rewards through `$heor-economic-inputs`. Target only exact `/strategies/<id>/state_costs/<index>` or `/strategies/<id>/state_utilities/<index>` scalars. Costs require non-negative DSA bounds and Gamma, Lognormal, or non-negative bounded Uniform PSA. Utilities require DSA bounds inside `[-1,1]` and Beta or Uniform inside `[-1,1]`. Bind all three fixed PSM artifacts by SHA-256. List every `<strategy>.pfs` and `<strategy>.os` curve as an omitted parameter with rationale. Classify the result as `partial_parameter_uncertainty` with scope `economic_inputs_only`; never call it a complete PSM PSA. Report the three duration scenarios separately; survival parameter covariance, curve choice, extrapolation, and joint PFS/OS uncertainty remain release blockers.
13. For partitioned-survival analysis `0.12.0` with uncertainty `0.12.0`, copy `assets/partitioned-survival-joint-uncertainty.template.json` and use `$heor-joint-survival-uncertainty` to validate the hash-bound manifest and JSONL rows. Bind the duration artifact in `partitioned_survival_inputs` and the two joint artifacts in `joint_survival_inputs`. Keep the same economic target rules as `0.11.0`; each PSA iteration consumes one complete joint curve row plus sampled economic inputs. Do not list represented PFS/OS curves or modeled treatment-effect duration as omitted. Explicitly omit curve-family selection and extrapolation assumptions; report duration alternatives as separate deterministic structural scenarios rather than folding them into the base PSA. Classify the output as `joint_curve_draw_parameter_uncertainty` with scope `joint_survival_curves_and_economic_inputs`; do not call it complete structural uncertainty.
14. State the independence rationale and resolve known omitted correlations. When evidence supplies a supported joint log-scale correlation estimate for economic inputs, declare only the admitted bounded Cholesky group. Do not estimate, repair, or guess a correlation matrix. Joint survival dependence comes from each complete draw row, not from these economic-input groups. List PSA omissions with a reason; omission is visible, not silently converted to fixed certainty.
15. Set an explicit unsigned 64-bit seed, 1,000–10,000 iterations, at least two increasing convergence checkpoints ending at the iteration count, and probability MCSE/drift thresholds no greater than 0.1. For schema `0.12.0`, iteration count must equal the joint draw count. The first-party desktop caps iterations because it returns auditable per-draw output; larger production runs require a future streamed result format, not an unbounded response.
16. Declare 2–101 unique increasing, non-negative decision thresholds and a rationale. Include the analysis plan's primary willingness-to-pay value. Derive the range from an explicit decision context or human instruction; never invent a jurisdictional threshold.
17. Define at least one bounded structural scenario with ordinary allowlisted replacements and a rationale. Under schemas `0.8.0` through `0.12.0`, restrict scenarios to state cost or utility scalars, discount rates, or half-cycle correction. Reject cycle count/length, transition matrix/schedule, life-table, relative-effect, hazard-ratio, survival curves, review-basis, operation, and transformation-internal replacements.
18. Bind `base_analysis.content_sha256` to the final exact bytes of `heor/analysis-plan.json`. Never upgrade one side of a required schema pair without the other.
19. Run `scripts/validate_uncertainty_plan.py`. For schemas `0.11.0` and `0.12.0` with PSM `0.4.0`, also pass `--partitioned-survival-plan heor/partitioned-survival-plan.json --survival-curve-materializations heor/survival-curve-materializations.json --treatment-effect-duration heor/treatment-effect-duration.json`. For `0.12.0`, additionally pass `--joint-survival-uncertainty-manifest heor/joint-survival-uncertainty.json --joint-survival-draws heor/joint-survival-draws.jsonl` and run the dedicated joint-survival validator. The desktop repeats and extends both audits before approval or execution.

## Boundaries

- Never create approval events, accept analyst assumptions, or claim independent validation.
- Never change identifiers, reference-case status, evidence, approvals, or file paths through an uncertainty target.
- Do not report a seeded run as generally converged merely because it completed. Report its checkpoint diagnostic and thresholds.
- Do not treat DSA as a substitute for joint PSA in a nonlinear model.
- Do not present schema `0.11.0` economic-input-only results or schema `0.12.0` joint-draw results, CEAC/CEAF, or conditional EVPI as complete structural PSM uncertainty. Fixed curves block `0.11.0`; curve choice, extrapolation, source-model validity, independent validation, and probabilistic integration across duration alternatives still block `0.12.0`.
- Do not use beta or Dirichlet for event rates, change a derived probability row, or describe independent draws as correlated. Only evidence-bound lognormal members may enter a correlation group; gamma, beta, uniform, Dirichlet, cross-group, singular, perfect, empirical-draw, copula, and rank-correlation dependence remain unsupported. Resolve known omitted correlations before review.
- Do not use transformation-parameter targets for curve fitting or selection, time-varying or non-proportional HR application, pooling, general CTMC intensities, competing risks, PFS/OS combination, clinical extrapolation validity, or transformation-space scenarios. RR/OR and constant-HR uncertainty are limited to their exact schema `0.9.0` and `0.10.0` targets and support above.
- Do not treat cost-effectiveness probability as a recommendation or policy threshold decision.
- Report CEAC and CEAF separately: a strategy's probability of being optimal is not always the probability that the strategy with the highest expected net benefit is optimal. Report multi-strategy ties separately; do not fractionally assign them without an explicit alternative method.
- Treat EVPI as a per-person upper bound over uncertainty represented in the current PSA only. Do not extrapolate it to a population without explicit eligible population, incidence/prevalence, time horizon, technology lifetime, and discounting inputs.
- Do not claim EVPPI, expected value of sample information, optimal study design, or a research-funding recommendation from this engine.
- Preserve unsupported distributions, correlations, and structural uncertainty as explicit blockers or limitations.

## Handoff

Report the analysis and uncertainty IDs, exact plan and uncertainty hashes, PSM and materialization hashes when applicable, joint manifest/draw hashes for schema `0.12.0`, PRNG algorithm/version, seed, parameter, correlation-group, threshold, and scenario counts, PSA iterations, CEAC/CEAF at the declared thresholds, primary-threshold per-person EVPI and its Monte Carlo standard error, omitted parameters, correlation handling and evidence bases, convergence result, blocking errors, and the next natural-language repair. For schema `0.11.0`, lead with `partial_parameter_uncertainty` / `economic_inputs_only` and the fixed-survival release blocker. For `0.12.0`, lead with `joint_curve_draw_parameter_uncertainty` / `joint_survival_curves_and_economic_inputs` and the remaining structural/source-validation blockers. Distinguish calculation reproducibility from methodological validity and leave population EVPI/EVPPI explicitly uncalculated.
