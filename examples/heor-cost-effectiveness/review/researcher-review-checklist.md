# Researcher review checklist

This checklist records questions for the researcher. It is not an approval
record and the application must not mark an item as approved on the
researcher's behalf.

## Decision problem

- Is the population defined for the intended decision?
- Are the intervention and comparator relevant?
- Are jurisdiction, perspective, horizon, outcomes, and intended use explicit?

## Conceptual model

- Do the three states adequately represent the disease and treatment pathway?
- Are irreversibility, absorbing death, cycle length, and half-cycle correction justified?
- Are important events, subgroups, treatment changes, or structural alternatives missing?

## Evidence and parameters

- Does every non-structural input have an admissible source and locator?
- Are transformations, units, price year, applicability, and uncertainty recorded?
- Are correlations and evidence-synthesis uncertainty represented where required?

## Analysis and validation

- Are base-case, deterministic, probabilistic, and structural analyses adequate?
- Have implementation checks and independent validation been completed?
- Are the results interpreted within their evidence and model limitations?

## Reporting and release

- Are assumptions, uncertainty, limitations, conflicts, and unresolved choices disclosed?
- Is the exact report version fit for its intended audience and permitted use?
