# Reporting plan

The local runner prepares `outputs/teaching-report.md` alongside the complete
JSON result. The draft report covers the decision problem, evidence status,
base case, deterministic sensitivity analysis, structural scenarios,
probabilistic teaching analysis, mechanical checks, Human review items, and
limitations.

The report must retain these boundaries:

- all numerical inputs and ranges are synthetic teaching assumptions;
- the illustrative value per QALY is not an official threshold;
- probability of positive incremental net monetary benefit is a calculation,
  not a decision;
- mechanical checks are not scientific or independent validation;
- no pricing, reimbursement, coverage, or policy conclusion is produced;
- release remains a Human responsibility for the exact artifacts.
