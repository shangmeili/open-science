# Evidence gap log

This complete teaching case contains no clinical, epidemiological, utility, or
unit-cost evidence. Every numerical value is a synthetic assumption used to
show the workflow. A real evaluation would need at least:

- a defined disease, jurisdiction, indication, and target population;
- comparative clinical-effect evidence and a justified transformation into
  model transition inputs;
- baseline natural-history and mortality evidence;
- treatment acquisition, administration, monitoring, adverse-event, disease
  management, terminal-care, and other perspective-relevant costs;
- preference-based health-state utility evidence and disutilities;
- price year, currency conversions, and inflation methods;
- parameter uncertainty derived from the admitted evidence rather than
  arbitrary teaching ranges;
- evidence-informed structural alternatives and model calibration targets when
  needed.

No gap is silently filled by a model provider. Replacing synthetic assumptions
with sourced inputs requires Human evidence selection and review.
