# Proposed decision problem

- Population: hypothetical adults with a progressive chronic condition
- Intervention: hypothetical new therapy
- Comparator: hypothetical current care
- Perspective: health-system payer
- Outcomes: total cost, life-years, and quality-adjusted life-years
- Model: closed cohort state-transition model
- States: stable, progressed, dead
- Cycle length: one year
- Time horizon: ten years
- Reward timing: average start- and end-of-cycle occupancy (trapezoidal half-cycle correction)
- Discounting: 5% annually for costs and outcomes, applied at the end of each cycle
- Currency and price year: CNY 2026
- Cohort size: 1,000 people
- Illustrative willingness-to-pay value: CNY 150,000 per QALY

Every field above is a proposed teaching assumption requiring Human review. The
willingness-to-pay value is not an official threshold and must not be used to
make a reimbursement or policy claim.
